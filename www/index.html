ComicTracker

Series with chapters you haven't read yet.

Search MangaDex

Manga, manhwa and manhua. These get automatic new-chapter checks.

Add from a website link

Paste the series page from the site you read on: Webtoons, MangaDex, or any other reading site. The tracker reads the title, cover and latest chapter, then checks that page for new chapters. If a site hides its chapter list, the series is saved with your link and you update chapters yourself.

Add manually

For series on sites without a feed. You update the latest chapter yourself.

Update checks

The tracker checks while server.py is running on this PC. Keep it running (or add it to Windows startup) to get notifications.

Use on your phone

To use the app on your phone, close this window and the black console window, then start the app with start-phone.bat instead of start.bat. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as this PC.

On your phone, open the camera and scan this code. It opens the app in your phone's browser. The phone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi as this PC, and the app must be running here.

Or type this link into your phone's browser. Keep it private: anyone with it can change your library.

Tip: in your phone browser's menu, choose Add to Home screen to open it like an app.
